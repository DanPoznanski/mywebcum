MacOS Sequoia

Install Anydesk and Allow Permissions

  1. Open https://anydesk.com

  1. Click-on Allow

  1. Double-click on icon

  1. Double-click on anydesk.dmg and drag Anydesk on Applications

  1. Double-click on icon Applications after double-click on icon Anydesk

  1. Click on Open

  1. Click on Allow

  1. Now need give permission for Screen Recording and Accessibility

  1. Switch it to ON and enter your password

  1. Click on Ouit & Reopen

  1. Now need give permission to Accessibility

  1. Switch it to ON

  1. All now Granted!

  1. Now can give address number to connect

DONE

 

Install FortiClient VPN and Allow Permissions

 

  1. Open https://www.fortinet.com and scroll down

  2. Fill all and download

  1. Allow it

  2. Click on it

  3. Double-click on FortiClientInstaller

  4. Click on Open

  1. Continue… Continue.. Agree.. Continue..

  1. Click Install, full password and click install software

  1. OK

  1. Allow

  1. Close

  1. Double-clik on icon FortiClient (upper corner)

  1. Press [ x ] and access

  1. Fill all 12.12.12.12 change to your IP and Save

  1. Connect

  1. Open Security & Privacy Settings

  1. General > Login items & Extensions > Network Extensions

  1. Fill your password

  1. Reconnect to VPN

Done!

 

Install Windows App

 

  1. Download and Install Windows App from Apple store https://apps.apple.com/us/app/windows-app/

  2. Open Windows App and Allow it

  1. Device > + > Add PC

  1. target machine ip and save

  1. Doble click on icon after add your credentials and connect

Done !
